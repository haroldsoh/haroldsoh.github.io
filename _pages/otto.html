Personal software · macOS

Your mail.
A little more calm.

Otto is a native Mac email client for reading, writing and organizing mail across Gmail and Microsoft 365 accounts.

Built by Harold Soh, for personal use
01 / MAIL

A focused inbox

Read conversations, search mail, manage attachments, and send from the account you choose. Keyboard shortcuts, snooze and a brief undo-send window help with everyday email.

02 / YOUR MAC

Direct connections

Otto connects to your mail provider from your Mac. Cached mail and drafts stay in local app storage. There is no Otto-operated mail relay or hosted mailbox.

03 / OPTIONAL AI

Help when you ask

Draft, revise or ask about an email using an AI service you configure. When invoked, that service receives the relevant text and any files you select.

A personal tool, with clear data practices.

Otto is not offered as a public email service. This site explains how it works and provides its privacy policy and terms of use. Google and Microsoft access is granted through their own sign-in screens.
