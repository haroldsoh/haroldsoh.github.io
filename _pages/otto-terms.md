Otto is a personal macOS software project by Harold Soh. It is currently maintained for his own use and is not offered as a public email service. These terms describe the intended use of the software; they do not create an account with Otto or replace your agreements with your mail provider.

## Use of the software

Use Otto only with accounts and information you are authorized to access. Follow your mail provider’s terms, applicable organizational requirements and the permissions of the people whose information appears in your email. You control which accounts are connected and which actions you take in the app.

## Email and optional services

Google, Microsoft, Apple Mail and any AI provider you configure are separate services or applications. Their access policies, quotas, retention practices and availability may affect Otto. You remain responsible for any service charges and for choosing suitable privacy and security settings. Otto does not provide or resell those services.

Review recipients, sending account, message text and attachments before sending. AI-generated text may contain errors or invented details; review and edit it before using it. Otto’s delayed-send cancellation works only before the message is handed to its provider. It cannot recall mail already submitted for delivery.

## Availability and reliability

Otto is an evolving personal project, provided as available without a promised service level, support response time or uninterrupted operation. Bugs and changes in third-party services can affect synchronization, reminders, formatting and delivery. Keep appropriate backups and use your provider’s own mailbox to check important or uncertain operations. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

## Privacy and stopping use

The [privacy policy](/otto/privacy/) explains the information Otto accesses, local storage, optional AI requests and deletion controls. You can stop using Otto, remove connected accounts and revoke its provider authorizations. Removing Otto does not itself delete mail held by your provider.

## Changes and contact

Features and these terms may change as the personal project develops. The date above identifies this version. For questions, [contact Harold Soh](https://haroldsoh.com/contact/).
